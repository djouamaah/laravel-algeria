# Third-party data

The files in `data/` are built by `tools/build.py` from the sources below.

## othmanus/algeria-cities

- Files: `tools/source-algeria-cities.json`, `tools/source-algeria-postcodes.json`
- Used for: wilayas, dairas, communes, postal codes
- Source: https://github.com/othmanus/algeria-cities (no license declared)

## Wikidata

- File: `tools/source-wikidata-communes.json`
- Used for: commune coordinates
- Source: https://www.wikidata.org (items that are instances of Q2989398, "commune of Algeria")
- License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)

## kossa/algerian-cities

- File: `tools/source-kossa-communes.json`
- Used for: commune coordinates (cross-checking)
- Source: https://github.com/kossa/algerian-cities
